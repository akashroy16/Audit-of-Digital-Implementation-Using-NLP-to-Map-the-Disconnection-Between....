
    FUTURE RECOMMENDATIONS & POLICY IMPLICATIONS
    ============================================
    Based on the analysis of 116 video titles.

    1. BRIDGING THE SUBJECTIVITY GAP:
       - Analysis shows local viral content is -36.5% more subjective 
         than international clinical data.
       - Future Work: Develop AI-driven tools for local health creators to align 
         emotional storytelling with clinical accuracy.

    2. COUNTERING VIRAL MISINFORMATION:
       - Recommendation: YouTube's algorithm should prioritize local content that 
         mimics the high engagement of viral videos but maintains a 'Subjectivity Score' 
         closer to clinical standards (target: < 0.12).

    3. PROMOTING 'ONE HEALTH' DIGITAL SOLUTIONS:
       - Recommendation: Integrate public health experts with software engineers to 
         create verified 'Addiction Recovery' dashboards in local languages.

    4. FUTURE RESEARCH DIRECTIONS:
       - Expand dataset beyond titles to include video transcript analysis for 
         deeper sentiment detection.
       - Conduct user surveys to understand why high-subjectivity titles 
         trigger more clicks in the Bangladeshi context.
